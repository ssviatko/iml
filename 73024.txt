
Width Specifiers

00 - byte
01 - word
10 - long (24 bits)
11 - bank (upper 8 bits of long word)

Format Specifiers

000 - Implied                   000t tttt oooo oooo

Implied Types

00000 - 01111                                                           Generic Implied
10000 - 11001                                                           Reserved
11010                           11010 0scc cccc                         Return, optionally on condition
11011                           11011 0scc cccc                         Execute next instruction conditionally
11100                           11100 0scc cccc sfff ffff ffff ffff     Call, optionally on condition, offset
11101                           11101 0scc cccc <abs24>                 Call, optionally on condition absolute
11100                           11100 0scc cccc sfff ffff ffff ffff     Branch, optionally on condition, offset
11101                           11101 0scc cccc <abs24>                 Branch, optionally on condition absolute
11110                           11110 eeee eeee                         Extended Instruction of type eeeeeeee Follows
11111                           11111 eeee eeee                         Extended Instruction of type eeeeeeee Follows

Conventional Instructions

001 - OPC.W Rx, (immediate)     001o oooo xxxo ooww
010 - OPC.W Rx, Ry              010o oooo xxxy yyww
011 - OPC.W Rx                  011o oooo xxx0 00ww
100 - OPC.W (Ax +/- OFFSET), Rx 100o oooo aaa0 00ww sfff ffff ffff ffff
101 - OPC.W Rx, (AX +/- OFFSET) 101o oooo aaa0 00ww sfff ffff ffff ffff
110 - OPC.W -(Ax), Rx           110o oooo aaar rrww
111 - OPC.W Rx, (Ax)+           111o oooo aaar rrww

Condition Specifiers

sccc cccc

s = 0: condition reset
s = 1: condition set

cccccc = 0000000: unconditional
cccccc = 0000001: carry
cccccc = 0000010: zero
cccccc = 0000011: negative

Example usage:

1C41 000F          SCOND(C) BREL +15     ;on condition carry set, branch relative +15
1B02 4046          CCOND(Z) MOV R2, R1   ;on not zero move R1 into R2
